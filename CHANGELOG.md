# Changelog

## 0.4.0 (1 Nov 2026)

### Added

- **`agribot_autonomy` package:** the row follower, fruit detector, drive node and harvest mission, moved out of `agribot_gazebo`. It has no Gazebo dependency and runs against any robot that provides the same topics. Vision, steering and mission decisions are pure functions (`vision.py`, `steering.py`, `mission_logic.py`), so they can be tested without ROS.
- **`agribot_interfaces` package:** `RowState` and `HarvestStatus` messages, the `VerifyPick` service and the `Harvest` action. A harvest is now an action goal with feedback, cancel and a result, sent by `harvest_client` or `ros2 action send_goal`.
- **Parameters:** `config/autonomy.yaml` sets every parameter. Each one is declared with a type, a range and a description. Out-of-range or inconsistent values stop the node at launch, and a running node rejects them on `ros2 param set`.
- **Launch split:** `sim.launch.py` (simulator), `autonomy.launch.py` (robot-side nodes) and `demo.launch.py` (both, plus filming). A missing or broken parameter file stops the launch.
- **Failure handling:** image, `cmd_vel` and startup watchdogs, stall detection, NaN rejection, corrupt-image tolerance, TF fallback, single-goal enforcement and cancel. A missing crate sensor counts a pick as attempted but not verified.
- **Tests:** 338 pass on Ubuntu (`colcon test`), covering pure logic, boundaries, failures, live nodes on a fast clock, launch, and the consistency of the bridge, controllers and URDF. The pure-logic tests also run on Windows without ROS.
- **`sim_cut_model`:** the simulated cut and crate check, split out of the mission. It serves `/crate/verify`.
- **Scenery:** drip lines, crate stacks, a fence and a tree line (`tools/sim/world_props.py`). They are visual only and tested to stay clear of the rover.
- **Docs:** [ros-architecture.md](docs/ros-architecture.md) covers the packages, nodes, interfaces, parameters, launch, failure behaviour and tests. RViz now shows the detected fruit and both debug images.
- **`tools/sim/ubuntu/sync.sh`:** copies the working tree to the Ubuntu laptop without committing.

### Changed

- **`urdf_fk.py`** moved to `agribot_autonomy`. It reads the URDF from `/robot_description` text, and IK uses a faster single-chain FK and several seeds.
- **Blade joint:** it starts just inside its open limit, because in DART a joint sitting exactly on its limit could stay locked and the first cut never closed.
- **`stop.sh`:** also stops the autonomy launch and every node of both packages.

### Results

- Filmed run: 3 of 3 picks verified in the crate (two ripe, one half-ripe). The fruit-centre estimates fell 16, 7 and 13 mm from the pedicel actually cut.
- Headless run: 1 of 1 picks verified.

### Known limits

- The video, GIF and stills are still from 0.3.0. The 0.4.0 screen recording caught other windows on the Ubuntu laptop, so it was not published.
- The colour rule still picks half-ripe fruit, and pedicel accuracy still needs to improve before the hardware (6 mm throat).

## 0.3.0 (1 Nov 2026)

### Added

- **Camera row-following** (`row_follower.py`): finds the bare-soil furrow in `/front_camera/image` and steers on a near and a far point of its centre line. Enabled through `/row_follower/enable`; publishes its state and a debug image.
- **Fruit detection** (`fruit_detector.py`): red blobs in the arm camera, ranged with the depth image and placed in the rover frame with TF. Publishes `/fruit/detections`, RViz markers and a debug image.
- **Perception-driven harvest mission** (`harvest_mission.py`, replacing `harvest_demo.py`): follows the row, stops at fruit in reach, confirms over 1.5 s, solves IK for the detected point, cuts, carries, drops and verifies. Clean run: 3 of 3 picks in the crate, with no targets from the world file.
- **Media:** new video, GIF, stills, camera and debug frames, ROS 2 graph and report in `docs/media`.
- **Provenance:** `docs/assets.md` records that every model except the credited SCARA base is original and generated by scripts in `tools/`.

### Changed

- **Steering:** a knuckle per front wheel instead of a swinging axle. Both knuckles are commanded, since Gazebo's ros2_control ignores `mimic`.
- **Drive node:** each wheel's speed comes from its distance from the turn centre; the yaw rate is recomputed from the clipped steering angle.
- **Filming:** `make_video.py` writes the MP4 to a temporary file and keeps the old one if processing fails; `film.sh` deletes the raw recording only after a good MP4.
- **`stop.sh`:** matches every node the demo starts, so no stale node survives into the next run.

### Known limits

- The colour rule picks half-ripe fruit too (two of the three clean-run picks).
- Fruit-centre estimates fell 6–24 mm from the pedicel cut; the real cutter's throat is 6 mm.
- The rover turns 30–60% as sharply as the bicycle model predicts (tyre slip); the camera loop compensates.

## 0.2.0 (1 Nov 2026)

### Added

- **`agribot_gazebo` package:**
  - Gazebo Fortress world with one harvest row, generated by `tools/sim/make_world.py`.
  - ros2_control: wheels, steering, arm (JointTrajectoryController), blade, trap door, scout pan.
  - Cameras (front, scout, arm RGB-D), IMU and ground-truth odometry.
  - ROS–Gazebo bridge.
  - Drive node (`cmd_vel` to a bicycle model) and harvest mission node.
- **Harvest mission:**
  - Drives to a ripe fruit, puts the cutter's throat on the pedicel by IK on the URDF, cuts, catches, carries and drops it in the crate.
  - Verifies each pick from Gazebo: 2 of 2 in the crate, 0.1 mm measured cutter error.
- **Collision groups:** the arm passes through fruit on the trusses, while the cradle and crate catch cut fruit.
- **Filming and evidence tools:** filming pipeline with a GUI camera director (`tools/sim/ubuntu/film.sh`, `make_video.py`), ROS graph drawing (`ros_graph.py`) and Ubuntu helper scripts.
- **Demo media:** `docs/media` holds the MP4, GIF, stills, camera frames, ROS 2 graph and report. `docs/simulation.md` covers results, how to run it, and limitations.

### Changed

- **Cutter:** soft lip raised to 16 mm above the foam; arm camera moved to a side mount at fruit height; trap-door axis now swings down.
- **Harvest crate:** now has collision (floor and walls).
- **SCARA Z axis:** damping 2 N·s/m, speed 0.1 m/s.
- **Fruit collision:** sphere sized by the vertical semi-axis; on-plant group `0x04`.

### Fixed

- **SCARA meshes:** extraction now clears stale files; one had put the gripper servo inside the wrist's collision hull.
- **Textured OBJ materials:** they rendered black in Gazebo for lack of `Kd`. The exporter fixes it and QA checks it.
- **Gazebo plugin path:** Fortress's `IGN_GAZEBO_SYSTEM_PLUGIN_PATH` is now set for gz_ros2_control.

## 0.1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

### Added

- **Project context:** plans, bills of materials, order lists, the control-board reverse-engineering notes and photos, the SCARA redesign, and the ROS 2 demo plan.
- **SCARA arm description:**
  - Split from the HowToMechatronics STEP into five links plus the stock gripper, in ROS frames.
  - Forward kinematics checked against the STEP to 0.002 mm.
  - Measured Z stroke: 249 mm.
  - Inertias estimated from mesh volumes.
- **Tomato cutter, concept v0:**
  - V mouth, 6 mm throat, hooked bypass blade, cradle and trap door.
  - RGB-D camera mounted on the cutter.
  - Blade and trap door are separate URDF joints.
- **Rover body:**
  - Stylised chassis at the measured size, with curved fenders, tread tyres, light bar and e-stop.
  - Front and rear camera pods, a scouting mast with pan head, and a harvest crate.
  - SCARA side mast with five pin heights.
- **Robot description:**
  - `agribot_description` ROS 2 package with xacro macros per subassembly and a top-level `agribot.urdf.xacro`.
  - xacro args: `tool:=cutter|gripper`, `mast_pin:=0..4`.
  - Expanded URDFs and an RViz display launch.
- **Tomato field:**
  - `agribot_field` ROS 2 package with Gazebo models and glTF copies.
  - Tomato fruit at three ripeness stages; origin at the knuckle, plus a cut-point frame.
  - Three staked plant variants, each with fruit-site lists.
  - Tileable raised mulch beds and a soil tile.
  - Field layout file and semantic labels.
- **Tooling:**
  - Asset generators: OpenCascade for the STEP, Blender for procedural assets and renders.
  - URDF generator with structural checks.
  - Minimal URDF FK/IK helper.
  - Renders in `docs/images`.

### Changed

- **SCARA mounting:** the SCARA base now runs along the rover's side. Pointing it outward put the base electronics box in the fruit zone.
- **SCARA Z stroke:** the measured 249 mm replaces the ~200 mm estimate in `docs/scara-redesign.md`.

### Not yet verified

- `colcon build`, RViz and Gazebo loading on Ubuntu 22.04 with ROS 2 Humble.
- Gazebo plugins: `ros2_control`, cameras, detachable joints, labels.
